# Feature guide

## The end-to-end workflow (the one from the brief)

| Step | What you do | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Open the app | Tap the icon at the start of your shift | **Home** shows today's top 3 (or suggestions), overdue items, Do First, follow-ups, due today, upcoming reminders and a short briefing |
| 2. Capture a task | Tap **+** (bottom-right on every tab) and say or type "Make sure dairy is cleaned up before Friday" | The preview shows *Task · Dairy · Due Thu · Q2 Schedule (“before Friday” → due Thu)*. Tap **Save**. |
| 3. Assign a quadrant | Open **Matrix** and drag the task onto Q1, or long-press › Move to Quadrant | The quadrant is saved as a manual choice and recorded in the task's history |
| 4. Record an associate request | **+** then "Sarah needs help understanding her schedule. Talk to her tomorrow." | Becomes a **Follow-up** for Sarah, due tomorrow, with the request recorded. Because Sarah is new, the preview asks you to confirm before adding her. |
| 5. Schedule a reminder | "Call the electrician tomorrow at 2:30pm", or set it in the task's **Reminder** field | A local notification is scheduled for 2:30 PM |
| 6. Receive a notification | — | The banner arrives even if the app is closed. Long-press for **Mark Complete / Snooze 15 Minutes / Snooze 1 Hour / Tomorrow Morning / Open Task**. |
| 7. Complete a responsibility | Tap the circle on any row, swipe right, or use the widget checkbox | It's marked complete and logged. For a follow-up, the associate's history shows it resolved. |
| 8. Review the weekly planner | Home › calendar icon › **Week** | Seven days (your week start, Saturday by default) with quadrant counts per day. Drag tasks between days. |
| 9. Use End My Shift | Home › **End My Shift** | Shows completed, unfinished, overdue, open commitments, unconfirmed hand-offs, upcoming deadlines, Q2 work and suggested priorities for tomorrow |
| 10. Carry unfinished tasks into your next shift | **Carry all to next shift**, or per item **Next shift / Pick date / Done** | They move to your next scheduled shift, skipping days off. Each item keeps its time of day and reminder offset, and postponements are counted. Starred suggestions become that shift's top 3. Nothing is discarded. |

## Your store and your schedule

**Store vocabulary.** Capture understands how a store team talks:
- **Roles:** "the store manager" or "the SM"; the fresh, food, fashion, AP, ACC, front end, digital/OPD, stocking and overnight coaches; "the fresh TL", "my TLs", "team leads".
- **Areas:** Stocking, Overnight, Fresh, Food, Fashion, Front End, Digital (OPD), Asset Protection (AP) and Auto Care Center (ACC). These also appear first in every department picker, with sub-departments like Dairy and Produce below them.
- **Store manager requests** count as important in the Matrix.

**Delegating to team leads.** Say it the way you'd say it on the floor, and the hand-off shows in the preview:

| You say | Result |
|---|---|
| "Have Jordan work the truck by end of shift" | A task handed to Jordan, due at your shift's end |
| "Ask the fresh TL to check dates in dairy" | Handed to "Fresh TL" |
| "Tell my TLs to work the steel at 2" | Handed to your TLs, as a group (no contact is created) |
| "Delegate the price change audit to Avery" | Handed to Avery |
| "Ask Maria about her schedule" | A **follow-up** you owe Maria, not a hand-off |

A handed-off task shows as **Waiting** until you confirm the person took it on, because it's never assumed accepted. You can also delegate from any task (long-press › **Delegate…**). The picker lists your team leads first, then coaches and the store manager, then associates. End My Shift lists hand-offs that haven't been confirmed.

**Store terms in Quick Capture.** Capture also understands everyday store language:

| You say | Result |
|---|---|
| "Have Stock 2 work the remix tonight" | Handed to the Stock 2 team (Stocking, freight); no contact is created |
| "Ask the O/N TL to finish the FDD freight" | Handed to the O/N TL (Overnight) |
| "Tell the MOD the baler is full" | Linked to the Manager on Duty (lowercase "mod" stays a modular) |
| "Dept 94 mod set by Friday" | Department: Produce (department numbers work as "dept 94", "department 94" or "D94") |
| "Nil picks are up, review FTPR with Avery Friday" | Digital (OPD), important (performance) |
| "Pre-use inspection on the walkie stacker" | Safety, always important |
| "Temp logs and expiration pull on the meat wall" | Compliance, always important; Meat |
| "Market is coming Thursday" | Important (leadership visit) |

Teams and groups (Stock 1–3, CAP 1–3, "the overnight team", "my TLs", "the stocking TLs") can be handed work, but they never become contacts.

**Store Terms reference.** Tap the book icon on Home (or Settings › Store terms) to search about 500 terms, acronyms and slang. They're grouped into 15 categories, including stocking, OPD, Front End, Fresh, metrics, safety and AP, equipment, scheduling, layout and department numbers. Each term is tagged when it's an **older term**, **informal** or **varies by store**. A list shows words with more than one meaning, such as MOD, BOB, DA and Pick Walk. A task's page explains any acronyms in it under **Terms**, and Siri answers "Look up a store term in Second Brain". The list is compiled from public employee sources and isn't official, so confirm anything that matters locally.

**Season Planner.** Open it from Settings › Season planner, or tap **Coming up** on Home, which shows the next three weeks along with the Walmart fiscal week, such as "FY27 · Week 43". It covers the next 12 months:
- **Holidays**, worked out by the calendar: Valentine's Day, Easter, Mother's Day, Memorial Day, Father's Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Halloween, Thanksgiving, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas Eve, Christmas, New Year's Eve and New Year's Day.
- **Announced sales events:** Walmart Deals (June 22–28, 2026), Early Black Friday Deals (Nov 12–15), Black Friday Deals (Nov 23–29; in stores from Nov 27 at 6 AM) and Cyber Monday Deals (Nov 30–Dec 2). Events for later years are added once Walmart announces them. Dates are never guessed.
- **Typical seasonal timing**, labeled "confirm locally": Lawn & Garden, back-to-school, the Halloween set just after Labor Day, the holiday conversion after Halloween, and post-holiday clearance.
- **Operations and safety:** the new fiscal year (Saturday of the week containing Feb 1), the two clock changes (overnight shifts run an hour shorter or longer), holiday staffing, summer heat, hurricane season and winter weather.

Each item has prep steps, such as "Black Friday safety walk: aisles clear, PLE parked, crowd plan". Tap **Add** to make one a task, or **Add all** and confirm. Due dates move back to your last scheduled workday before the event, and reminders follow your shift start. Swipe an item to hide it if it doesn't apply to your store. You can turn the Home card off in Settings.

Quick Capture also understands holidays after "by", "before", "on", "until" or "ahead of": "by Black Friday" or "before Thanksgiving". A bare name like "the Halloween set" or "Christmas trees" is never treated as a date.

**Recent changes in Store Terms.** A new category covers:
- Vizio, onn., SmartCast and Walmart Connect
- Digital shelf labels
- Emerging Store Manager (Store Lead is now marked as an older term)
- Market Coordinator, an eliminated role
- AI task management and real-time translation in the associate app
- Walmart Deals and Black Friday Deals, Walmart+ and Scan & Go
- Rapid remodels and "Summer Frights"

Quick Capture sends Vizio and onn. to Electronics, and treats digital shelf labels as merchandising work rather than the Digital (OPD) area.

**Privacy nudges.** If what you type in Quick Capture, a task, a note or a person's context looks like disciplinary detail ("write up", "DA"), attendance records ("NCNS", "attendance points"), medical or leave detail ("FMLA", "LOA"), an AP case, a password or WIN, or personal identifiers, a short note suggests keeping only the task and leaving the details in the approved system. It never blocks saving and never changes your text.

**People by role.** Associates groups people as Store Manager, Coaches, Team Leads and Associates (with job titles like Stocker or Cashier), each with their area. To add a whole team at once, use **Add Several People**: choose a role and area, then enter one name per line. A team lead's page also shows the tasks handed to them that are still waiting.

**Your weekly schedule.** In Settings › Your schedule (also part of the welcome steps), set each day to **7–5, 10–8, 12–10**, a custom time, or **Off**. Second Brain then:
- **Matches phrases to your shifts:** "by end of shift" means 5 PM on a 7–5 day and 8 PM on a 10–8 day; "before I leave" works too. "First thing" means the start of your next shift, and "next shift" skips your days off.
- **Times reminders to your shifts:** date-only items get a reminder at that day's shift start.
- **Briefs you only on workdays:** the daily briefing arrives at the start of each scheduled shift.
- **Flags days off:** you get a heads-up when something is due on a day you're off.
- **Carries work to your next shift:** End My Shift moves unfinished work there, so items aren't dropped onto a day off. The Home header shows today's shift, or your next one on days off.

## Your role and imported notes

**Your role.** In Settings › Your role (also part of the welcome steps), pick **Coach**, **Team Lead** or **Store Manager**, your main area (for example Food), and any other areas you also cover. Other areas are hidden by default, and you can change this at any time. Your role then shapes:
- **Home:** a **My area** card shows open work for your areas and store-wide items. Store managers see the whole store, so they don't get this card.
- **Tasks:** a **My area** filter.
- **Season Planner:** a toggle shows only your areas plus store-wide items. It's on by default and also applies to the Home "Coming up" card.
- **Suggested routines:** a starter list of common routines for your role and area, such as a Saturday numbers review for coaches or a truck plan for Stocking. Add one, or **Add all** after confirming. They become ordinary recurring tasks you can edit, pause or stop. They're suggestions only, so follow your store's actual schedule.

**Scan, photo and walk notes.** Import also takes a **Scan** of a printed or handwritten page (on an iPhone with a camera) or a **Photo** of notes or a whiteboard. The text is read on your iPhone, and photos aren't saved. **Speak walk notes** (also **Walk notes** on Home) lets you talk as you walk the floor, like "Aisle 12 has holes. Dairy cooler door is leaking." Each sentence becomes an item, and walk notes keep every area you mention.

**Import notes.** Copy an email or notes (for example a market manager's follow-up in Outlook), then tap **Import** on Home, the Import button in Notes, or Settings › Import notes, and tap **Paste**. You can also use Siri or Shortcuts ("Import notes into Second Brain"). Second Brain:
- **Finds action items:** it reads bullet points and action sentences, skips greetings, sign-offs and the quoted earlier email, and uses the subject line.
- **Routes each item:** using area headings ("Food", "Fresh:"), inline labels ("Front End - …") and the departments mentioned, it sorts items into **For you**, **Total store** and **No area mentioned**. Items for other areas are hidden, with a count shown. "Dairy" or "Frozen" count as Food, "Produce" or "Bakery" as Fresh, and so on.
- **Uses only stated dates:** an item gets a due date only if the notes give one ("by Wednesday"). Nothing is invented.
- **Lets you review first:** your items and total store items start ticked, and you can untick or edit any of them. Nothing saves until you tap **Add**. Each task records where it came from, and you can choose to keep the original text as a note.

Copy and paste only content you're allowed to keep on a personal device. If your company's Outlook blocks copying, that's company policy and the app doesn't try to work around it. The privacy note still appears if pasted text looks like personnel, medical or case details.

## Ask

Tap **Ask: what do I do if…** on Home (or Settings › Ask) and type a question, like "The freezer door won't open, only manually" or "We're short-staffed tonight, what first?" Ask **suggests guides** that match. It doesn't write its own answers, so it can't make up steps that don't fit your store.

- **Guides (offline):** short, vetted guides across Safety, Leading people, Running the floor, Customers, and Ethics & conduct, including equipment and freight problems such as a freezer door that won't open on its own, breaking down a frozen and dairy truck, broken equipment, and a cooler running warm. They're built from general leadership and safety practice and public sources: OSHA, FDA/USDA/CPSC recalls, the National Weather Service, and Walmart's public policies and Code of Conduct. Before you ask, Ask suggests guides for your role. Where a store procedure matters, a guide says to follow it and who to check with.
- **Your own guides:** under **Your guides**, tap **Write a guide for your store** and add the steps your store trained you on (for example "Baler won't start"). Ask shows them first when they match. Don't include passwords, codes or anyone's personal details.
- **Your store's steps:** each built-in guide also has a spot for your store's own steps, kept on your phone.
- **Emergencies:** if a question sounds like an emergency (someone hurt, a weapon, smoke), Ask shows "call 911 now, then get the manager on duty" first.
- **Search online:** **Search Reddit (r/walmart)** and **Search the web** open in your browser. Reddit posts are other people's opinions, not company policy, and the app doesn't read Reddit itself.
- **Turn steps into tasks:** tap **+** next to a step and confirm to add it to your tasks.

## Huddle

Tap **Huddle** on Home for a 5-minute script built from today:
- **Safety:** a safety message, seasonal when it matters (Black Friday week, wet entrances in winter, heat in summer). Tap **Another safety message** for a different one.
- **Yesterday:** what got done.
- **Today's top three:** with the team lead each item is handed to.
- **Shout-outs:** type names and why.
- **Questions:** a closing prompt.

Read it from your phone, **Share** it with your team leads, or **Save to Notes**. Shout-outs are kept only if you save.

## Shift timeline

Tap **Timeline** on Home to see your shift hour by hour: your plan blocks plus any tasks with a due time. The suggested plan has a check-in, huddle, floor walk, a main block for your area, lunch, follow-ups, a second walk, projects, and End My Shift.
- **Edit my plan:** change titles, start times and lengths. Blocks are placed from the start of your shift, so one plan works for 7–5, 10–8 and 12–10.
- **Nudges:** turn on **Nudge me when each block starts** for a quiet notification at each block today and next shift.
- **Home:** while you're on shift, Home shows **Now: [block] · until [time]**.

## Team lead lists

In **Associates**, tap the clipboard icon (top left), or go to **Settings › Team lead lists**. Each team lead (anyone whose role contains "lead") shows how many handed-off tasks are open.
- **Hand off tasks:** pick tasks and confirm. They show as waiting until the person confirms.
- **Send list:** texts them just the task titles and due times.
- **Take back:** swipe a task to return it to you.

## Weekly recap

In **Weekly Review**, **Share recap with your store manager** sends a short summary: tasks done (and how many were safety items), highlights, what's still overdue, and follow-up counts. It doesn't name associates.

## Apple Watch

The Second Brain watch app shows your shift's time left, how many tasks are done, and your top three. Tap one and confirm to mark it done on your iPhone. **Quick note** takes dictation, Scribble or the keyboard and saves exactly what you said to Notes on your iPhone. If the phone is out of range, the watch queues changes and sends them when it reconnects. The iPhone app does all the saving.

## Off duty (Focus filter)

In iOS **Settings › Focus**, choose a Focus such as Personal or Sleep, then **Focus Filters › Add Filter › Second Brain › Off duty**. While that Focus is on, widgets show "Off duty", Home says work can wait, and the shift Live Activity is removed. Nothing is deleted or rescheduled.

## Floor walk

Tap **Floor walk** on Home. You get a checklist for each of your areas (from Your role):
- **Safety first:** exits and fire equipment, spills, equipment put away, pallets off the main aisles.
- **Then the floor:** holes, features and endcaps, signs, mods, zoning and cleanliness.
- **Area checks:** for example out-of-date product for Food and Fresh, top stock and the backroom for Stocking, and entrances and registers for Front End.

Tap ✓ if a check is fine or ! if it needs work, and add a note if you like. Your progress is kept for the day if you leave. **Finish walk** lists what needs work, safety first. Untick anything you don't want, then add the rest as tasks. Safety items go to Do First, and no dates are added. You can also save a walk summary to Notes.

## Shift handoff

At the bottom of **End My Shift**, **Share handoff** sends a short summary by text, email or any app. It lists what's still open (safety and Do First first), what's coming up, and what got done, plus your "Notes for tomorrow". Associate follow-ups stay out unless you turn on **Include associate follow-ups**.

## Severe weather alerts

In **Settings › Severe weather alerts**, turn alerts on and set your store's location: tap **Use my current location** at the store, or enter its ZIP code.
- **Home banner:** warnings and severe alerts from the National Weather Service (weather.gov) show as a banner. Tap it for what NWS says to do, with links to the severe weather guide and weather.gov.
- **Notifications:** you're notified from an hour before your shift until it ends, once per new alert.
- **Privacy:** only the store's approximate location (rounded to about 100 m) is sent to weather.gov.
- **Limits:** iOS decides when background checks run, so this doesn't replace Wireless Emergency Alerts or your store's emergency plan.

## Shift on the Lock Screen

When you open the app from 30 minutes before your shift until it ends, your shift appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. It shows time left, how many of today's tasks are done, any overdue items, and your top three priorities, and it updates as you complete tasks. Turn it off in Settings › **Shift on Lock Screen**. iOS ends Live Activities after about 8 hours, so on a 10-hour shift it may disappear before the shift ends; open the app to start it again.

## Quick Note (Action button)

Set it up in your iPhone's **Settings › Action Button › Shortcut › Second Brain › Quick Note**. Press the button anywhere, type or dictate, and the note is saved to Notes exactly as you said it, without opening the app. It's tagged "quick note", and nothing is turned into a task or given a date. You can also say "Quick note in Second Brain". If the note looks like it holds personnel, medical or other sensitive details, Siri adds a short heads-up.

## Quick Capture

- **Where:** the **+** button on all five tabs, the Home quick actions (Capture, Task, Follow-up, Note, Reminder), the Control Center control or Action button, Siri ("Add a task to Second Brain"), and the widget's Capture link.
- **Input:** the keyboard, the keyboard's dictation key, or the in-app **mic** button. The mic uses Apple Speech and runs on-device when supported.
- **Interpretation (on-device, no internet):** title, details, type (task, follow-up, reminder, issue, note, meeting note, idea), associate (matched against your list, or detected from phrases like "check with…" or "Sarah needs…"), roles ("the overnight coach"), department, due date and time, reminder, recurrence, category, priority and Eisenhower quadrant with reasons.
- **Never invents things:** with no date there's no due date. A new person, a vague date ("next week"), a time without AM/PM, a time that already passed, or a reminder with no time all show a **Check before saving** box. The button changes to **Confirm & Save**. Return saves instantly only when nothing is ambiguous.
- **Adjust:** tap **Adjust** to change any field. Fields you change stay as you set them while you keep typing.
- **Inbox:** saves exactly what you typed, with no dates or people applied. Sort it later with one tap from Tasks › Inbox, where you see the suggestion before applying it.

Examples it understands: *tomorrow, tonight, Friday, next Monday, before Friday, by end of shift, first thing, in 30 minutes, at 3, 2:30pm, Dec 4, 12/4, the 15th, in 3 days, this week, next week, every day, every weekday, every Mon and Thu, every 2 weeks on Tuesday, on the 1st of every month, remind me…, idea:, note:, meeting notes:*.

## Eisenhower Matrix

- **Urgency and importance are scored separately.** Deadlines, hazards and urgent wording raise *urgency*. Safety, compliance, commitments to people, development, performance results, leadership requests and accountability wording raise *importance*.
- **A close deadline never makes something important by itself.** "Reprint labels, due today" is Q3 Delegate.
- **Safety and compliance are always important**, even when due months away. Moving one to Q3 or Q4 asks for confirmation.
- **Overview:** a 2×2 grid with counts and the first four items, filtered by All open, Today or This week. Drag between quadrants. Tap a quadrant to open a swipeable full-screen pager with swipe actions.
- **Due-soon banner:** when time makes a task urgent, the app *suggests* moving it and you confirm each one.
- **Q4:** "Archive all" asks first. Nothing is deleted or archived automatically.
- **Q3:** **Delegate…** records the person and marks the task *Waiting* until you confirm they accepted.
- **Why?** Every task shows the reasons for its suggested quadrant, plus **Use suggestion** after a manual override.

## Associate follow-ups

- Capture naturally, or use **Associates › + › New Follow-up**: person, what they asked, what you committed to, conversation date, optional follow-up date and reminder, and priority.
- Sections: **Overdue · Today · Upcoming · No date · Waiting on someone · Resolved**. Search across names, requests and commitments.
- **People:** a list with open-follow-up badges (red when overdue). A person's page shows open follow-ups, other tasks, notes, **interaction history** (requests, conversations, resolved items and logged talks) and resolved follow-ups.
- **Related tasks:** from a follow-up, **Add related task** links next steps to it.
- A privacy reminder appears wherever people information is entered: it's a personal organizer, so don't store medical, disciplinary, password or confidential personnel information.

## Tasks

Fields: title, description, type, status (Inbox, Not Started, In Progress, Waiting on Someone, Completed, Deferred, Archived), priority (Critical, High, Normal, Low), category, urgency and importance (shown as the quadrant), due date and time, reminder, recurrence, associate, department, delegation, follow-up details, subtasks (convertible to tasks), linked notes, calendar link, completion notes and history.

Filters: Today, Upcoming, Inbox, Overdue, Waiting, No Date, Completed, Archived. Search covers titles, details, people, departments, requests and commitments.

Row actions: swipe right to complete. Swipe left for **Tomorrow** or **Reschedule**. Long-press for Complete, Snooze, Reschedule, Move to Quadrant, Top 3, Delegate, Open, Duplicate, Archive and Delete (with an "Archive instead" option).

**Unfinished tasks never disappear when a new day begins.** They become overdue and stay at the top.

## Planning

- **Daily planner:** a day strip (yesterday through +6 days). Choose **three main priorities**, with ranked suggestions (★). Sections for Q1, Q2 scheduled work, follow-ups, other tasks, recurring, upcoming reminders, calendar events (optional) and completed.
- **Weekly planner:** seven days from your chosen week start, with per-day quadrant counts and completed and event counts. Drag a task onto another day, or long-press › Move to. Also lists unfinished work from earlier, outstanding associate requests and operational issues.

## Notes

Types: general, associate, meeting, department, operational, safety, idea. Each note has a title, body, checklist (tap ↗ to turn an item into a task), tags, department, associate link, pinning, linked tasks and **Create task from this note**. Search covers titles, bodies, tags, people and checklist text.

**Find follow-ups** scans the note for commitments and action items ("I'll get back to Maria Friday", "- [ ] order pallets", "Action item: …"). Each suggestion is shown first and is only created when you choose.

## Recurring tasks

Daily, weekdays, specific weekdays, every N days/weeks/months, and monthly on a given day (clamped to short months). Each occurrence is a real task (seriesID + date key). The app generates a week ahead, never creates duplicates, and remembers deleted occurrences. **Pause / Resume / Discontinue** remove only future occurrences you haven't touched. History is kept. Manage them in Tasks › ⋯ › Recurring Tasks or Settings.

## Notifications

- Types: **Reminders**, **deadline alerts** (at a timed due date), **still-open alerts** (an hour after a missed deadline, or at shift end for date-only items) and a **daily briefing** at shift start.
- Actions: **Mark Complete · Snooze 15 Minutes · Snooze 1 Hour · Tomorrow Morning · Open Task**. In the app you can also snooze 30 minutes or pick any date and time.
- **Offline and reliable:** everything is scheduled with iOS ahead of time. The full schedule is recomputed whenever data changes, the app opens, an action runs, or iOS grants a background refresh. iOS keeps at most 64 pending alerts, so the app keeps the soonest 60.
- **Privacy:** "Hide task details in notifications" replaces titles with a generic message.
- **Permission:** explained in onboarding and requested only once. If you decline, Settings shows a link to iOS Settings.

## Widgets

- **Small:** today's most important task and the open and due counts.
- **Medium:** top 3 priorities with **tap-to-complete checkboxes**, the next reminder, counts and a Capture link.
- **Large:** up to 5 tasks with checkboxes, Eisenhower counts and follow-ups.
- **Lock Screen:** circular (priority count), rectangular (next reminder or today's primary task) and inline.
- **Control Center / Action button:** Quick Capture.

Widgets read the same database through the App Group. They refresh after every change, at the next reminder, at midnight, or every 30 minutes, within iOS's refresh budget.

## Siri & Shortcuts

"Add a task to Second Brain" · "Remind me in Second Brain" · "What are my priorities in Second Brain" · "Show my overdue tasks in Second Brain" · "Show my follow-ups in Second Brain" · "End my shift in Second Brain" · "Quick capture in Second Brain" · "Quick note in Second Brain" · "Import notes into Second Brain" · "Look up a store term in Second Brain" · "What's next in Second Brain".

**Control Center:** add **Quick Capture**, **Floor Walk** and **Walk Notes** buttons from the Control Center editor. You can also put them on the Lock Screen or the Action button. Spoken captures are interpreted on-device. If anything is ambiguous, Siri saves it to your Inbox and tells you why.

## Intelligence (optional, on-device)

Smart capture, Eisenhower suggestions, follow-up detection, the daily briefing, the weekly review (repeatedly postponed tasks, stale inbox items, long waits, items that may no longer be needed) and Top 3 suggestions all run with built-in rules. You don't need an account, a subscription or an internet connection. On iOS 26 devices with Apple Intelligence, **Apple Intelligence briefing** can rephrase the briefing more naturally. Nothing changes without your confirmation.

## Calendar (optional)

Turn it on in Settings › Calendar. Events then appear in the daily planner and the weekly counts, and tasks that overlap an event show a warning. **Add to Apple Calendar** on a task creates an event only when you tap it. Tasks are otherwise kept separate. If you revoke access, the app shows the status instead of failing.

## Privacy & security

There's an optional Face ID or passcode lock, plus a privacy blur in the app switcher when the lock is on. You can hide notification previews. Data is stored with iOS data protection, and you can export or restore it. There are no accounts, no analytics, no employer systems and no credentials.
