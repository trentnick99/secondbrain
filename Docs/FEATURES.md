# Feature guide

## The end-to-end workflow (the one from the brief)

| Step | What you do | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Open the app | Tap the icon at the start of your shift | **Home** shows today's top 3 (or suggestions), overdue items, Do First, follow-ups, due today, upcoming reminders and a short briefing |
| 2. Capture a task | Tap **+** (bottom-right on every tab) and say or type "Make sure dairy is cleaned up before Friday" | The preview shows *Task · Dairy · Due Thu · Q2 Schedule (“before Friday” → due Thu)*. Tap **Save**. |
| 3. Assign a quadrant | Open **Matrix** and drag the task onto Q1, or long-press › Move to Quadrant | The quadrant is saved as a manual choice and recorded in the task's history |
| 4. Record an associate request | **+** then "Sarah needs help understanding her schedule. Talk to her tomorrow." | Becomes a **Follow-up** for Sarah, due tomorrow, with the request recorded. Because Sarah is new, the preview asks you to confirm before adding her. |
| 5. Schedule a reminder | "Call the electrician tomorrow at 2:30pm", or set it in the task's **Reminder** field | A local notification is scheduled for 2:30 PM |
| 6. Receive a notification | — | The banner arrives even if the app is closed. Long-press for **Mark Complete / Snooze 15 Minutes / Snooze 1 Hour / Tomorrow Morning / Open Task**. |
| 7. Complete a responsibility | Tap the circle on any row, swipe right, or use the widget checkbox | It's marked complete and logged. For a follow-up, the associate's history shows it resolved. |
| 8. Review the weekly planner | Home › calendar icon › **Week** | Seven days (your week start, Saturday by default) with quadrant counts per day. Drag tasks between days. |
| 9. Use End My Shift | Home › **End My Shift** | Shows completed, unfinished, overdue, open commitments, unconfirmed hand-offs, upcoming deadlines, Q2 work and suggested priorities for tomorrow |
| 10. Carry unfinished tasks into tomorrow | **Carry all to tomorrow**, or per item **Tomorrow / Pick date / Done** | Each item keeps its time of day and reminder offset. Postponements are counted. Starred suggestions become tomorrow's top 3. Nothing is discarded. |

## Quick Capture

- **Where:** the **+** button on all five tabs, the Home quick actions (Capture, Task, Follow-up, Note, Reminder), the Control Center control or Action button, Siri ("Add a task to Second Brain"), and the widget's Capture link.
- **Input:** the keyboard, the keyboard's dictation key, or the in-app **mic** button. The mic uses Apple Speech and runs on-device when supported.
- **Interpretation (on-device, no internet):** title, details, type (task, follow-up, reminder, issue, note, meeting note, idea), associate (matched against your list, or detected from phrases like "check with…" or "Sarah needs…"), roles ("the overnight coach"), department, due date and time, reminder, recurrence, category, priority and Eisenhower quadrant with reasons.
- **Never invents things:** with no date there's no due date. A new person, a vague date ("next week"), a time without AM/PM, a time that already passed, or a reminder with no time all show a **Check before saving** box. The button changes to **Confirm & Save**. Return saves instantly only when nothing is ambiguous.
- **Adjust:** tap **Adjust** to change any field. Fields you change stay as you set them while you keep typing.
- **Inbox:** saves exactly what you typed, with no dates or people applied. Sort it later with one tap from Tasks › Inbox, where you see the suggestion before applying it.

Examples it understands: *tomorrow, tonight, Friday, next Monday, before Friday, by end of shift, first thing, in 30 minutes, at 3, 2:30pm, Dec 4, 12/4, the 15th, in 3 days, this week, next week, every day, every weekday, every Mon and Thu, every 2 weeks on Tuesday, on the 1st of every month, remind me…, idea:, note:, meeting notes:*.

## Eisenhower Matrix

- **Urgency and importance are scored separately.** Deadlines, hazards and urgent wording raise *urgency*. Safety, compliance, commitments to people, development, performance results, leadership requests and accountability wording raise *importance*.
- **A close deadline never makes something important by itself.** "Reprint labels, due today" is Q3 Delegate.
- **Safety and compliance are always important**, even when due months away. Moving one to Q3 or Q4 asks for confirmation.
- **Overview:** a 2×2 grid with counts and the first four items, filtered by All open, Today or This week. Drag between quadrants. Tap a quadrant to open a swipeable full-screen pager with swipe actions.
- **Due-soon banner:** when time makes a task urgent, the app *suggests* moving it and you confirm each one.
- **Q4:** "Archive all" asks first. Nothing is deleted or archived automatically.
- **Q3:** **Delegate…** records the person and marks the task *Waiting* until you confirm they accepted.
- **Why?** Every task shows the reasons for its suggested quadrant, plus **Use suggestion** after a manual override.

## Associate follow-ups

- Capture naturally, or use **Associates › + › New Follow-up**: person, what they asked, what you committed to, conversation date, optional follow-up date and reminder, and priority.
- Sections: **Overdue · Today · Upcoming · No date · Waiting on someone · Resolved**. Search across names, requests and commitments.
- **People:** a list with open-follow-up badges (red when overdue). A person's page shows open follow-ups, other tasks, notes, **interaction history** (requests, conversations, resolved items and logged talks) and resolved follow-ups.
- **Related tasks:** from a follow-up, **Add related task** links next steps to it.
- A privacy reminder appears wherever people information is entered: it's a personal organizer, so don't store medical, disciplinary, password or confidential personnel information.

## Tasks

Fields: title, description, type, status (Inbox, Not Started, In Progress, Waiting on Someone, Completed, Deferred, Archived), priority (Critical, High, Normal, Low), category, urgency and importance (shown as the quadrant), due date and time, reminder, recurrence, associate, department, delegation, follow-up details, subtasks (convertible to tasks), linked notes, calendar link, completion notes and history.

Filters: Today, Upcoming, Inbox, Overdue, Waiting, No Date, Completed, Archived. Search covers titles, details, people, departments, requests and commitments.

Row actions: swipe right to complete. Swipe left for **Tomorrow** or **Reschedule**. Long-press for Complete, Snooze, Reschedule, Move to Quadrant, Top 3, Delegate, Open, Duplicate, Archive and Delete (with an "Archive instead" option).

**Unfinished tasks never disappear when a new day begins.** They become overdue and stay at the top.

## Planning

- **Daily planner:** a day strip (yesterday through +6 days). Choose **three main priorities**, with ranked suggestions (★). Sections for Q1, Q2 scheduled work, follow-ups, other tasks, recurring, upcoming reminders, calendar events (optional) and completed.
- **Weekly planner:** seven days from your chosen week start, with per-day quadrant counts and completed and event counts. Drag a task onto another day, or long-press › Move to. Also lists unfinished work from earlier, outstanding associate requests and operational issues.

## Notes

Types: general, associate, meeting, department, operational, safety, idea. Each note has a title, body, checklist (tap ↗ to turn an item into a task), tags, department, associate link, pinning, linked tasks and **Create task from this note**. Search covers titles, bodies, tags, people and checklist text.

**Find follow-ups** scans the note for commitments and action items ("I'll get back to Maria Friday", "- [ ] order pallets", "Action item: …"). Each suggestion is shown first and is only created when you choose.

## Recurring tasks

Daily, weekdays, specific weekdays, every N days/weeks/months, and monthly on a given day (clamped to short months). Each occurrence is a real task (seriesID + date key). The app generates a week ahead, never creates duplicates, and remembers deleted occurrences. **Pause / Resume / Discontinue** remove only future occurrences you haven't touched. History is kept. Manage them in Tasks › ⋯ › Recurring Tasks or Settings.

## Notifications

- Types: **Reminders**, **deadline alerts** (at a timed due date), **still-open alerts** (an hour after a missed deadline, or at shift end for date-only items) and a **daily briefing** at shift start.
- Actions: **Mark Complete · Snooze 15 Minutes · Snooze 1 Hour · Tomorrow Morning · Open Task**. In the app you can also snooze 30 minutes or pick any date and time.
- **Offline and reliable:** everything is scheduled with iOS ahead of time. The full schedule is recomputed whenever data changes, the app opens, an action runs, or iOS grants a background refresh. iOS keeps at most 64 pending alerts, so the app keeps the soonest 60.
- **Privacy:** "Hide task details in notifications" replaces titles with a generic message.
- **Permission:** explained in onboarding and requested only once. If you decline, Settings shows a link to iOS Settings.

## Widgets

- **Small:** today's most important task and the open and due counts.
- **Medium:** top 3 priorities with **tap-to-complete checkboxes**, the next reminder, counts and a Capture link.
- **Large:** up to 5 tasks with checkboxes, Eisenhower counts and follow-ups.
- **Lock Screen:** circular (priority count), rectangular (next reminder or today's primary task) and inline.
- **Control Center / Action button:** Quick Capture.

Widgets read the same database through the App Group. They refresh after every change, at the next reminder, at midnight, or every 30 minutes, within iOS's refresh budget.

## Siri & Shortcuts

"Add a task to Second Brain" · "Remind me in Second Brain" · "What are my priorities in Second Brain" · "Show my overdue tasks in Second Brain" · "Show my follow-ups in Second Brain" · "End my shift in Second Brain" · "Quick capture in Second Brain". Spoken captures are interpreted on-device. If anything is ambiguous, Siri saves it to your Inbox and tells you why.

## Intelligence (optional, on-device)

Smart capture, Eisenhower suggestions, follow-up detection, the daily briefing, the weekly review (repeatedly postponed tasks, stale inbox items, long waits, items that may no longer be needed) and Top 3 suggestions all run with built-in rules. You don't need an account, a subscription or an internet connection. On iOS 26 devices with Apple Intelligence, **Apple Intelligence briefing** can rephrase the briefing more naturally. Nothing changes without your confirmation.

## Calendar (optional)

Turn it on in Settings › Calendar. Events then appear in the daily planner and the weekly counts, and tasks that overlap an event show a warning. **Add to Apple Calendar** on a task creates an event only when you tap it. Tasks are otherwise kept separate. If you revoke access, the app shows the status instead of failing.

## Privacy & security

There's an optional Face ID or passcode lock, plus a privacy blur in the app switcher when the lock is on. You can hide notification previews. Data is stored with iOS data protection, and you can export or restore it. There are no accounts, no analytics, no employer systems and no credentials.
